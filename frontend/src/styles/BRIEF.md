# Design brief — "Focused, editorial, quietly premium"

1. **Mood:** a well-set study desk at night — calm paper surfaces, confident
   serif headlines, mono details; feels like a serious tool that respects
   your time, not a hype product.
2. **References:** Exercism's clarity, Linear's restraint, Stripe Docs'
   typography, editorial print layouts (asymmetric grids, hairline rules).
3. **It is NOT:** neon-on-dark, glassmorphism, glowing purple orbs, emoji
   icons, three identical feature cards, or "Supercharge your potential".
4. **Color:** warm-tinted paper neutrals + ONE ink-blue accent (`--accent`)
   + semantic ok/warn/bad; every subject gets a small accent mark (dot/bar),
   never a full gradient. Light and dark are both first-class.
5. **Craft rules:** hairline borders + layered low shadows, 4px grid,
   tabular numbers for scores/timers, Lucide icons only, shape+color+label
   for status, and specific human microcopy everywhere.
